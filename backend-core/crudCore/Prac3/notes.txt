CINEMA BOOKING SYSTEM:
Design a cinema booking system. A movie can play in multiple theaters, theaters have seats, and users can book seats for a particular show

#What are the entities?
User

Movie
   ↓
Show
   ↓

   ↓
Seats

#But that isn't enough.
Because:

Seat A1

could be:

AVAILABLE for 6 PM show
BOOKED    for 9 PM show

Therefore we need:

ShowSeat

---------------------------------------

Why ShowSeat exists
This is the interview trap.

Suppose:
Seat A1
exists.

There are three shows:

Show 1 → 6 PM
Show 2 → 9 PM
Show 3 → tomorrow 6 PM

You cannot store:

Seat A1
status = BOOKED

because:

A1 might be booked for Show 1
but available for Show 2

Therefore:

ShowSeat
-------------------------
showId    seatId    status
S1        A1        BOOKED
S2        A1        AVAILABLE
S3        A1        AVAILABLE

🔥 This is the key design insight.
